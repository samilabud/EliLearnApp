# Privacy Policy — Animal Adventures

**Last updated: 18 September 2026**

Animal Adventures is made by EliLearn Apps for young children. This policy explains, in plain language, exactly what the app does and does not do with information.

## We do not collect personal information

The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no name or email field, and no way to type free text. It does not ask for, collect, or transmit any personal information about you or your child.

We do not collect or use:

- Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or photographs
- Location
- Contacts, calendar, microphone, or camera
- Advertising identifiers or any other persistent device identifier for advertising

## What the app stores, and where

The app saves a small amount of information **on your device only**, so that your child does not lose their place:

- Game progress (current level, the cards on the board, score)
- Which animals your child has met, for the animal album
- Settings you choose, such as language, sound on or off, and the daily play limit
- Anonymous counts of in-app activity — for example how many times a game was opened — used to understand which parts of the app are used

None of this is sent anywhere. It stays in the app's private storage and is deleted when the app is uninstalled. The anonymous activity counts are not linked to any identity and never leave the device.

## Information that does leave the device

We want to be precise rather than sweeping, so there is one thing worth naming.

The app uses Expo's update service to deliver fixes without a full store update. When the app starts, it contacts Expo's servers (`u.expo.dev`) to ask whether a newer version is available. That request includes technical details — the app version, the platform, and an installation identifier generated by Expo. It does not include any personal information about you or your child, and it is not used for advertising or profiling.

Expo's own privacy policy is at <https://expo.dev/privacy>.

## No advertising

The app shows no advertising of any kind, and contains no advertising SDKs.

## No purchases

There is nothing to buy in the app. There are no in-app purchases and no subscriptions.

## No links out of the app

The app contains no links to websites, social media, or other apps. A child cannot navigate out of it.

## Children's privacy

Animal Adventures is designed for children and is intended to comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families policy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, because we do not collect personal information from anyone.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date at the top. If a change ever means the app begins collecting information it does not collect today, we will say so clearly rather than quietly editing the text.
